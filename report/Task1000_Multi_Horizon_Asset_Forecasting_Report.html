Task 1000: Multi-Horizon Asset Price Forecasting

Full Project Report

This report documents the complete workflow of Task 1000, from historical market data preparation and exploratory analysis to financial feature engineering, multi-horizon forecasting, model comparison, uncertainty analysis, and final price estimates.

The project focuses on Tesla (TSLA), Alphabet (GOOGL), Bitcoin (BTCUSD), Ethereum (ETHUSD), and QQQ. The analysis is designed to compare equity and cryptocurrency market behaviour across multiple forecasting horizons.

1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to forecast the future prices of TSLA, GOOGL, BTCUSD, and ETHUSD across seven horizons: 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years, and 10 years.

Two forecasting approaches will be compared: ARIMA as a traditional time-series benchmark and Random Forest Regressor as a feature-driven machine learning model.

The project also incorporates momentum and mean reversion analysis, volatility regime analysis, and Monte Carlo simulation to assess forecast uncertainty and cross-asset differences.

2. Data Sources

Historical daily market data were collected from the following sources:

  • TSLA: Stooq
  • GOOGL: Stooq
  • QQQ: Stooq
  • BTCUSD: Stooq
  • ETHUSD: CoinMarketCap

The datasets contain daily Open, High, Low, Close, and Volume information where available. BTCUSD from Stooq does not include a Volume field, so volume-based features are treated as optional for that asset.

4. Exploratory Data Analysis

The purpose of the EDA stage is to identify major differences in historical price behaviour, return characteristics, volatility, and cross-asset relationships before constructing predictive features and developing the forecasting models.

4.1 Historical Price Analysis

This section examines the long-term price behaviour of the four forecast assets and the QQQ benchmark.

4.1.1 Tesla (TSLA)

TSLA traded at relatively low price levels with gradual appreciation between 2010 and 2019. A clear regime shift appeared around 2020, when the stock entered a much stronger upward trend accompanied by substantially larger price swings.The post-2020 period was characterized by repeated rallies toward new highs followed by substantial corrections. The larger and more frequent price swings suggest higher forecasting uncertainty and a greater likelihood of regime-dependent behaviour.

1.2 Alphabet (GOOGL)

GOOGL showed steady growth over the long term, despite a temporary drawdown between 2022 to 2023. Its price path is relatively smooth, with a noticeable acceleration in the upward trend after 2024. Compared with TSLA, GOOGL exhibits a more persistent and smoother long-term trend.

1.3 Bitcoin (BTCUSD)

BTCUSD traded at low price level for an extended period before it entered a more pronounced growth phase around 2017-2018. After 2018, its price fluctuated and encountered several bloom-and-bust cycle. BTCUSD exhibits strong cyclical behavio. Therfore, forecasting BTCUSD mat require models that account for changing markert regimes, particularly across long horizons.

1.4 Ethereum (ETHUSD)

ETHUSD remained at relatively low price levels during its early years before entering a major growth phase around 2020–2021.This was followed by a substantial drawdown in 2022, after which the asset gradually recovered and entered another strong upward phase toward 2025–2026.Compared with BTCUSD, ETHUSD shows similarly cyclical behaviour but with more pronounced reversals between expansion and correction phases.These repeated boom-and-bust patterns suggest that ETHUSD forecasting is likely to be highly regime-dependent, particularly over longer horizons.

1.5 QQQ Benchmark

QQQ exhibited a relatively persistent long-term upward trend, with gradual appreciation over most of the sample period. The most notable interruption occurred around 2022, when the index experienced a clear drawdown before recovering and accelerating again from 2023 onward. Compared with TSLA and the cryptocurrency assets, QQQ follows a smoother and more continuous price path with fewer extreme regime shifts.This suggests that trend persistence and momentum-based features may provide more stable information for forecasting QQQ-related market movements.

4.1.4 Cumulative Return Analysis

Cumulative returns provide a standardized comparison of long-term investment performance across assets with different absolute price levels. For each asset, the analysis assumes an initial investment of $1 on the first available trading day in its historical dataset and tracks how that investment would have grown over time.

Key Observations

BTCUSD shows the strongest long-term wealth creation among the selected assets, meaning that an initial $1 investment at the beginning of its available historical period would have grown substantially more than in the other assets. ETHUSD follows a broadly similar growth pattern, with rapid appreciation accompanied by substantial drawdowns. By comparison, GOOGL and QQQ exhibit smoother and more persistent long-term growth paths, while TSLA shows greater fluctuations within the equity group.

Log-Scale Interpretation

A logarithmic scale makes it easier to compare cumulative growth paths across assets with substantially different return magnitudes. On a conventional linear scale, the much larger cumulative growth of BTCUSD and ETHUSD would compress the equity series near the bottom of the chart, making their long-term movements difficult to observe. The log scale therefore provides a clearer comparison of proportional growth across all five assets.

Risk-Return Implications

The higher cumulative growth of BTCUSD and ETHUSD is accompanied by larger price fluctuations and deeper drawdowns, indicating a more pronounced risk-return trade-off. GOOGL and QQQ demonstrate more stable long-term wealth accumulation, while TSLA occupies an intermediate position, with lower fluctuations than the cryptocurrencies but greater volatility than the other equity assets.

4.1.6 Cross-Asset Correlation Analysis

Daily return correlations are used to examine the extent to which the selected assets move together and to identify potential diversification relationships across asset classes.

Equity Relationships

Within the equity group, GOOGL shows the strongest relationship with QQQ, with a correlation of 0.70, indicating that its daily returns move relatively closely with the broader growth-oriented equity benchmark. TSLA has a moderate correlation of 0.50 with QQQ, while its correlation with GOOGL is lower at 0.33. This suggests that TSLA is influenced by broader market conditions but also exhibits more idiosyncratic price behaviour.

Cryptocurrency Relationships

BTCUSD and ETHUSD have a correlation of 0.51, indicating a moderate positive relationship between the two cryptocurrency assets. This suggests that they are partly driven by common crypto-market conditions, while still retaining asset-specific return behaviour.

Equity vs Cryptocurrency

Correlations between the equity and cryptocurrency groups are generally low, ranging from approximately 0.10 to 0.25. These weaker cross-asset relationships suggest potential diversification benefits, since equity and cryptocurrency returns do not consistently move together. From a forecasting perspective, the differences in correlation structure also indicate that market-relative features may have different predictive value across asset classes.

4.1.7 Overall EDA Findings

The exploratory analysis highlights clear differences in growth patterns, risk characteristics, and cross-asset relationships across the selected assets. Cryptocurrency assets generally exhibit faster long-term wealth creation but also experience larger drawdowns, stronger volatility regimes, and more pronounced extreme returns. The equity group follows comparatively smoother growth paths, although TSLA remains substantially more volatile than GOOGL and QQQ.

  • Growth Pattern: BTCUSD and ETHUSD show stronger long-term growth but also more pronounced boom-and-bust cycles and deeper drawdowns. GOOGL and QQQ exhibit smoother and more persistent long-term growth, while TSLA shows greater fluctuations within the equity group.
  • Risk Structure: Cryptocurrency returns display wider dispersion, more substantial tail risk, and more frequent extreme observations. Rolling volatility also shows repeated transitions between low- and high-volatility regimes across all assets, indicating that market risk is time-varying rather than constant.
  • Cross-Asset Relationships: GOOGL and QQQ show the strongest return correlation among the selected assets, while BTCUSD and ETHUSD exhibit a moderate positive relationship. Correlations between equities and cryptocurrencies remain relatively low, suggesting potential diversification benefits across asset classes.
  • Forecasting Implications: The EDA suggests that forecasting features should be sensitive to the characteristics of each asset rather than applied uniformly. Trend and momentum-based features may provide more stable information for GOOGL and QQQ, while rolling volatility and volatility regime indicators may be particularly important for TSLA, BTCUSD, and ETHUSD. Differences in cross-asset correlation also suggest that market-relative features may have different predictive value across assets.
